## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CV evidence | Describe truthful actions and outcomes. | Write a specific example from any relevant responsibility. |
| Applications | Match evidence to the role criteria. | Draft a targeted paragraph for a sample advert. |
| Interviews | Prepare examples and questions. | Answer a question with situation, action, and result. |
| Professional contact | State purpose, relevant facts, and requested next step. | Draft a short email and phone opening. |
| Workplace behaviour | Clarify expectations, report problems, and respect boundaries. | Respond to an unclear instruction professionally. |
| Evaluating jobs | Check organisation, pay, hours, costs, and access. | List verification questions before accepting. |
| Payslips | Reconcile gross pay, stated deductions, and net pay. | Identify a mismatch without guessing tax law. |
| Employment terms | Read responsibilities and exit conditions; check local guidance. | Identify unanswered offer terms. |
| Experience | Use projects, volunteering, or responsibilities honestly. | Choose a small demonstrable project. |
| Development | Identify a skill gap and feedback route. | Plan a short practice and review cycle. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `work.foundation` | Identify job requirements and write one truthful evidence statement. | An advert annotated with matching evidence. |
| Applied | `work.applied` | Use a structure to prepare an application and interview answer. | A targeted paragraph and practised answer. |
| Independent | `work.independent` | Evaluate a fictional offer and reconcile its payslip before asking clear questions. | An independent offer review and pay calculation. |
| Advanced Scenario | `work.advanced-scenario` | Adapt to unclear hours, an access need, or a changed offer without inventing entitlements. | A professional clarification and appropriate advice route. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
