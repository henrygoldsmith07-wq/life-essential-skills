# 07 · Digital safety: skills and mastery

[Short guide](../guides/07-digital-safety.md) · [Scenario S07](../scenarios/07-digital-safety.md) · [Map](../curriculum/dependency-map.md)

## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passwords | Use separate strong credentials for important accounts. | Review your main-email protection without recording the secret. |
| Password managers | Understand trusted storage and recovery arrangements. | Explain how you would securely manage distinct passwords. |
| MFA | Enable an offered second factor and plan recovery. | Describe recovery without exposing codes. |
| Phishing and scams | Verify unexpected requests through independent channels. | Handle a realistic suspicious payment message. |
| Backups | Keep recoverable copies of important files. | Restore a harmless sample file. |
| Privacy | Limit unnecessary permissions and sensitive sharing. | Review a sample app permission request. |
| Device security | Use updates and appropriate device access protection. | Find update and lock settings. |
| Recovery | Use the official provider process if access is lost. | Draft an account-recovery sequence. |
| Safe downloads | Use trusted sources and avoid unexpected executable attachments. | Explain a safer route to the needed document. |
| Identity theft response | Contact relevant providers and local official reporting channels promptly. | Prepare a response outline without promising a guaranteed outcome. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `digital-safety.foundation` | Explain why email, unique passwords, updates, and MFA matter. | An account-protection checklist with no secrets. |
| Applied | `digital-safety.applied` | Use official settings to review account protection and restore a sample backup. | A private settings review and restored sample. |
| Independent | `digital-safety.independent` | Handle a suspicious message without clicking its link or sharing codes. | An independent verification and payment-hold sequence. |
| Advanced Scenario | `digital-safety.advanced-scenario` | Adapt after a possible account compromise or realistic impersonation. | Official recovery, relevant provider contact, and local reporting steps. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
