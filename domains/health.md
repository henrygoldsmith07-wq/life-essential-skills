## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Healthcare access | Identify routine, urgent, and emergency routes locally. | Choose a verified route without diagnosing symptoms. |
| Sleep | Plan an achievable wind-down and sleep opportunity. | Trial a routine and record barriers privately. |
| Food routines | Plan varied food suited to needs and resources. | Write a realistic meal outline. |
| Movement | Find appropriate guidance for your age and circumstances. | Plan an accessible activity without prescribing treatment. |
| Hygiene | Build basic personal and food-hygiene routines. | Demonstrate a routine with appropriate instructions. |
| Medicine safety | Follow directions and ask a pharmacist or clinician about uncertainty. | Draft a question about a missed dose without improvising a dose. |
| Appointments | Prepare questions, transport, and follow-up reminders. | Create an appointment checklist. |
| Health information | Check authorship, evidence, date, and relevance. | Identify why a social post is insufficient for treatment decisions. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `health.foundation` | Identify routine, urgent, and emergency help routes from official local guidance. | Correctly labelled routes and their sources. |
| Applied | `health.applied` | Use a checklist to prepare a routine and appointment questions. | A feasible routine and concise appointment checklist. |
| Independent | `health.independent` | Handle a missed appointment and an uncertain medicine instruction by contacting appropriate professionals. | An independent contact plan without changing treatment. |
| Advanced Scenario | `health.advanced-scenario` | Evaluate a persuasive health claim and adapt practical arrangements for an access barrier. | Verification steps, an accessible alternative, and clear limits. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
