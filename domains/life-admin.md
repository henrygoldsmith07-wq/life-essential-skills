## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Records | Keep an inventory in secure private storage. | Locate sample records without sharing identity data. |
| Bills and renewals | Track amounts, dates, and verified contacts. | Build a dated reminder list. |
| Appointments and forms | Read requirements and prepare questions. | Complete a fictional form using only required data. |
| Contracts | Identify cost, term, responsibilities, and exit conditions. | Annotate a sample agreement. |
| Insurance | Compare cover, exclusions, excess, and renewal. | Ask about an unclear exclusion before relying on cover. |
| Complaints | State facts, evidence, desired remedy, and next step. | Draft a concise complaint using verified channels. |
| Subscriptions | Check cancellation route and confirmation. | Plan notice and follow-up for a fictional subscription. |
| Official correspondence | Verify sender and deadline independently. | Separate a real responsibility from an unverified payment demand. |
| Professional phone/email | Give purpose and facts, ask a clear question, record the outcome. | Write an opening script and a follow-up note. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `life-admin.foundation` | Identify an agreement's cost, duration, responsibility, and cancellation questions. | An annotated fictional agreement. |
| Applied | `life-admin.applied` | Use a checklist to organise records, reminders, and verified contacts. | A private inventory and dated action list. |
| Independent | `life-admin.independent` | Resolve the billing scenario with correct totals and a documented clarification or complaint. | An independent email and next-step timeline. |
| Advanced Scenario | `life-admin.advanced-scenario` | Adapt when cancellation is disputed or correspondence is suspicious, using current local advice. | An evidence log, verified advice route, and safe follow-up. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
