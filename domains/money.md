## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | Use take-home income and all planned outgoings. | Balance a fictional month and label any gap. |
| Cash flow | Order payments and receipts by date. | Calculate the lowest balance before payday. |
| Bank accounts | Compare access, fees, and applicable protection using official guidance. | List questions before choosing an account. |
| Debit and credit | Distinguish using available money from borrowing. | Explain repayment obligations in a sample purchase. |
| Borrowing | Compare total repayment, fees, duration, and exit conditions. | Calculate and explain two fictional offers. |
| Debt | Identify affordability gaps and legitimate advice routes. | Prepare an adviser question list without inventing a repayment rule. |
| Buffer and saving | Choose a realistic goal and consider access. | Revise saving after an unexpected expense. |
| Payslips and tax | Distinguish gross pay, deductions, and net pay; check current local rules. | Reconcile a fictional payslip and statement. |
| Insurance | Read cover, exclusions, excess, premium, and renewal terms. | Explain an exclusion and a question for the provider. |
| Scams | Verify financial requests independently. | Decline to pay while checking a suspicious request. |
| Investing basics | Understand risk, fees, diversification, and possible loss. | Explain why a promised guaranteed return needs scrutiny. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `money.foundation` | Create an arithmetically balanced monthly budget from fictional income and costs. | Income, allocations, and unallocated amount. |
| Applied | `money.applied` | Use a checklist to allow for irregular costs and calculate dated cash flow. | Dated running balance and an irregular-cost allowance. |
| Independent | `money.independent` | Handle a surprise cost without assuming funds exist or neglecting essentials. | A revised budget and a legitimate support route if needed. |
| Advanced Scenario | `money.advanced-scenario` | Compare credit offers by total repayment, timing, risk, and unanswered terms. | Calculations plus an affordability and contract explanation. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
