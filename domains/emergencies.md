## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contacts | Find the official emergency and non-emergency routes. | Give a dispatcher a fictional location and incident summary. |
| Household plan | Plan exits, contacts, meeting points, and access needs. | Walk through a plan that works without internet. |
| Fire and flood | Find local authority instructions before an incident. | Identify a safe help route without entering a hazard. |
| Power outage | Plan light, communication, and essential equipment support. | Adapt a plan when phones and lifts are unavailable. |
| Professional help | Separate everyday problems from immediate danger. | Explain when to stop and seek qualified help. |
| First-aid pathway | Find recognised practical training suited to your household. | Record a suitable course and the skills it will assess. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `emergencies.foundation` | Identify an appropriate emergency help route and explain why scene safety comes first. | A fictional call outline and verified local contact. |
| Applied | `emergencies.applied` | Use a checklist to make an offline household plan including access needs. | A plan with exits, meeting point, contacts, and named gaps. |
| Independent | `emergencies.independent` | Respond to the outage scenario with a safe sequence and verified support routes. | An unprompted action sequence that avoids entering hazards. |
| Advanced Scenario | `emergencies.advanced-scenario` | Adapt the plan for a failed phone and an additional access need; identify professional limits. | A revised plan with a fallback and escalation trigger. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
