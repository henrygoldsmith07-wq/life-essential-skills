## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overload | Notice your own early signs without self-diagnosing. | Name two signs and a manageable next action. |
| Stress | Try an everyday restorative activity. | Record whether it helped and what to change. |
| Support network | Map trusted people and professional routes. | Identify who can help with which need. |
| Seeking help | Explain the difficulty and request specific support. | Draft a short request to a verified service. |
| Healthy routines | Protect basic needs when planning a demanding week. | Reduce a plan to feasible essentials. |
| Problem breakdown | Separate an overwhelming situation into controllable steps. | Choose one action instead of treating every problem as immediate. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `wellbeing.foundation` | Identify overload signs and distinguish a manageable task from a need for more support. | A specific observation and next action. |
| Applied | `wellbeing.applied` | Use a prompt to build a private support plan and divide a problem into steps. | Named support roles and one realistic first step. |
| Independent | `wellbeing.independent` | Respond to the overloaded-week scenario with a feasible plan and a clear support request. | A plan that protects basic needs and contacts appropriate help. |
| Advanced Scenario | `wellbeing.advanced-scenario` | Adapt when the first contact is unavailable and identify when urgent professional help takes priority. | A verified fallback route without diagnosis or treatment advice. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
