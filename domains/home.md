## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meals and shopping | Check supplies, compare unit prices, and plan a short list. | Plan meals within a fictional food budget. |
| Food safety | Follow cleaning, separation, cooking, and storage guidance. | Explain the handling steps for the sample ingredients. |
| Cooking | Choose suitable equipment and follow instructions. | Prepare a simple meal with appropriate supervision if needed. |
| Laundry | Read fabric and machine labels. | Sort a fictional load and explain the wash choice. |
| Cleaning | Follow labels, ventilate as directed, and avoid mixing products. | Write a safe product-use checklist. |
| Routines | Share tasks and plan a manageable schedule. | Allocate dishes, cleaning, laundry, and rubbish. |
| Maintenance | Identify responsibility and competence limits. | Distinguish a routine task from qualified repair. |
| Utilities | Know verified reporting routes and safe limits. | Report a fictional outage without tampering with equipment. |
| Repairs | Describe the fault and keep a record. | Draft a concise repair request. |
| Moving | Plan supplies, meter/admin handover, transport, and access. | Make a moving-day checklist without assuming local legal rules. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `home.foundation` | Plan a simple meal and identify key food-safety and hazardous-repair limits. | A shopping list and safe task boundaries. |
| Applied | `home.applied` | Use instructions to prepare a meal and make a household checklist. | Practical meal evidence and a feasible routine. |
| Independent | `home.independent` | Handle the household scenario with a costed shopping plan and safe repair escalation. | Independent calculations and a repair request. |
| Advanced Scenario | `home.advanced-scenario` | Adapt routines to an equipment failure, move, or access limitation. | A revised plan with a workable safe alternative. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
