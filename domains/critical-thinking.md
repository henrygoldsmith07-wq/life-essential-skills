## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source verification | Find original evidence and check context and date. | Trace a claim to its source or label it unverified. |
| Statistics | Find denominators, absolute amounts, and comparison groups. | Recalculate a relative and absolute change. |
| Causation | Look for alternative explanations and study limits. | Explain why association alone does not establish a cause. |
| Manipulated content | Check provenance and independent reporting. | Identify why an image or recording alone is insufficient proof. |
| Persuasion | Notice urgency, social proof, and missing costs. | Rewrite a sales claim as a testable question. |
| Uncertainty | Separate known facts from assumptions. | State a conclusion and what would change it. |
| AI literacy | Treat generated text as a starting point; verify citations and protect data. | Check an AI answer against an original source without sharing secrets. |
| Decision journal | Record options, evidence, predictions, and a review date. | Reassess a decision after new information. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `critical-thinking.foundation` | Separate a claim, its evidence, and an assumption. | A labelled claim and a missing fact. |
| Applied | `critical-thinking.applied` | Use a checklist to trace a source and calculate relative and absolute differences. | Correct calculations and source notes. |
| Independent | `critical-thinking.independent` | Assess an unfamiliar social or AI claim using original evidence and independent verification. | An independent conclusion with limitations. |
| Advanced Scenario | `critical-thinking.advanced-scenario` | Adapt the conclusion to manipulated content or incomplete evidence and name what would change it. | A revised judgement that does not invent certainty. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
