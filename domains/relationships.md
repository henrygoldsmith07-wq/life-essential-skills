## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | Reflect back meaning and check understanding. | Summarise a reply without inventing motives. |
| Assertiveness | Separate observation, effect, and request. | Make a specific request that allows refusal. |
| Boundaries | State an action you can control. | Define what you will do if a request is not met. |
| Consent | Check agreement and respect refusal or changed decisions. | Explain why earlier agreement does not settle the current interaction. |
| Disagreement | Clarify needs and explore workable alternatives. | Propose an option without demanding agreement. |
| Difficult conversations | Choose an appropriate time and clear purpose. | Draft a short opening and follow-up. |
| De-escalation | Pause if safe and prioritise support when danger is present. | Explain why a threatening situation is not a routine negotiation. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `relationships.foundation` | Distinguish an observation from a motive claim and recognise that consent can change. | A rewritten statement and consent explanation. |
| Applied | `relationships.applied` | Use a script to make a request, reflect a reply, and define a boundary. | A short two-person role-play. |
| Independent | `relationships.independent` | Handle a realistic disagreement with listening, a clear request, and a workable boundary. | An unscripted written or spoken response. |
| Advanced Scenario | `relationships.advanced-scenario` | Adapt to refusal or escalating behaviour, choosing a safe pause or appropriate support. | A response that avoids coercion and recognises safety limits. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
