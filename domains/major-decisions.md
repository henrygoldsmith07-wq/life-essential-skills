# 12 · Major decisions and community: skills and mastery

## Small skills to practise

Choose one row at a time. The guide introduces the domain; these actions deepen it without requiring a long lesson.

| Subskill | Learn or practise | Demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Housing | Compare full cost, condition, access, and written terms. | List questions before paying or signing. |
| Transport | Plan timing, fares, access, and a disrupted-service alternative. | Choose a feasible route and backup. |
| Education | Check entry, accreditation where relevant, costs, and outcome claims. | Compare two courses and one small trial. |
| Major purchases | Include ongoing costs and repair/exit risks. | Compare total cost over a defined period. |
| Reversibility | Identify what can be tested or undone. | Choose a small experiment before a large commitment. |
| Local services | Identify the responsible organisation, not just its name. | Find a service that meets a practical need. |
| Civic literacy | Distinguish local, devolved, and UK responsibilities; check official participation rules. | Find the relevant body without making political assumptions. |
| Community resources | Locate libraries, advice, and accessible support. | Verify opening/contact details for a chosen service. |

## Observable mastery

| Stage | Competency ID | Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | `major-decisions.foundation` | Identify essential needs and full costs for two options. | A comparison with explicit assumptions. |
| Applied | `major-decisions.applied` | Use a checklist to compare housing, transport, and contract questions. | A costed comparison and verified local source. |
| Independent | `major-decisions.independent` | Choose between realistic options and plan feasible transport and an administrative next step. | An independent choice with a clear tradeoff. |
| Advanced Scenario | `major-decisions.advanced-scenario` | Adapt to changed income, service disruption, or incomplete terms and consider a reversible alternative. | A revised choice, missing-fact check, and fallback. |

Use the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) before awarding a stage. A written scenario demonstrates judgement and planning, not certification or physical competence. Record practical demonstrations separately where a criterion requires them.
