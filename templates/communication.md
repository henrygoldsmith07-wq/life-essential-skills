# Clear email and phone communication

[Work](../guides/09-work.md) · [Life administration](../guides/11-life-admin.md)

Use fictional information in the repository; keep real records private.

## Email

- Subject: specific purpose and non-sensitive reference if appropriate.
- Opening: who you are and why you are writing.
- Facts: dates, relevant amounts, or the issue, without unnecessary private details.
- Request: the clarification, action, or remedy you are seeking.
- Next step: ask how and when to follow up; do not invent a legal deadline.

```text
Subject: Clarification of duplicate subscription charge

Hello,
My fictional statement shows two charges of 9 for the same billing period.
Please explain the second charge and confirm the appropriate correction
if it was made in error. I can provide relevant evidence through your
verified secure process.
Please confirm the next step and expected response time.
Thank you.
```

## Phone

1. Find the contact through an independent official source.
2. Prepare a short purpose statement and two questions.
3. Ask what information is required and why; use the official secure process.
4. Note the date, contact/reference, answer, action, and follow-up date privately.
5. Ask for written confirmation of important agreements.

If the issue is unresolved, use the provider's complaint process and relevant local advice service. A clear complaint describes the problem and requested remedy; it does not establish that the remedy is legally owed.
