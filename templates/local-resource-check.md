Check the original publisher, current page status, and territorial scope. A UK website may describe England-only rules. A source being reachable does not mean its advice applies to your situation.

Record postcode, addresses, reference numbers, identity information, and health/financial details only in secure private storage. The repository's packs contain public service links, not personal records.
