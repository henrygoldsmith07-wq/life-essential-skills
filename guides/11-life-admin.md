# 11 · Documents, agreements, and life administration

[Curriculum](../README.md) · [Next: Major decisions](12-major-decisions.md)

**Goal:** find important records, understand a sample agreement, and track your responsibilities.

## Learn

Life administration becomes easier when you know what you need, where it is stored, and when it needs attention. Make a private inventory of identity records, housing documents, work agreements, insurance information, appointments, and renewal dates. An inventory can describe a storage location without containing sensitive document copies.

Before signing an agreement, identify the parties, purpose, price, duration, payment dates, cancellation process, penalties, and what each party must do. Write down anything you do not understand. Ask for clarification in writing and use qualified local advice where necessary. Do not assume a standard-looking agreement fits your situation.

Tax, benefits, employment rights, tenancy, and insurance rules vary by jurisdiction and can change. Use the relevant official local authority or qualified adviser for current rules. Keep a record of important conversations: date, reference number, agreed next action, and deadline.

## Example

A subscription costs 12 per month, but the agreement lasts a year. Its cost over that year is 144 before any additional fees. Check whether you can cancel early, when renewal happens, and how to give notice. The payment frequency alone does not tell you the commitment.

## Practise

1. Create a private checklist with columns: responsibility, next date, official contact, and storage location.
2. Add three real responsibilities or use fictional examples.
3. Read a sample agreement and identify its main costs and exit conditions.
4. Set reminders before relevant deadlines.
5. Find your local tax authority and a legitimate consumer or legal-advice service.

## Self-check

1. What should you understand before signing?
2. Why should you keep a record of an important service conversation?
3. Why is a guide from another country insufficient for local legal rules?

<details>
<summary>Suggested answers</summary>

1. Responsibilities, total cost, duration, deadlines, risks, and exit conditions.
2. It helps you follow up and document what was agreed.
3. Laws, procedures, eligibility, and protections differ and may change.

</details>

**Done when:** you can locate your records privately, explain a sample agreement, and identify your next administrative deadline.

**Local reference task:** record the official authorities for your own location in your private checklist. This guide intentionally does not state country-specific legal rules.
