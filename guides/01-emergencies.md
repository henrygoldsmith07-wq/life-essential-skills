# 01 · Emergencies and personal safety

[Curriculum](../README.md) · [Next: Money](02-money.md)

**Goal:** know how to get help and prepare your household before an emergency happens.

## Learn

Preparation reduces the number of decisions you must make under pressure. Know your local emergency number, how to describe your location, and how to contact someone you trust. Identify exits and an agreed meeting place. Account for mobility, communication, medication, and other access needs.

If an incident happens, first assess whether approaching would expose you to danger. Call local emergency services when needed, give the location and what happened, and follow the dispatcher's instructions. Do not enter fire, floodwater, traffic, or electrical hazards to investigate.

First aid is a hands-on skill. Arrange recognised first-aid and CPR/AED training that fits the people you may need to help. A written guide cannot assess your technique. Use your local emergency authority's advice for the hazards in your area and for household supplies.

## Example

Your household may lose power during storms. Agree where a torch is kept, how you will contact each other, what assistance anyone needs, and what you will do if phones cannot connect. Store the plan somewhere reachable without the internet.

## Practise

1. Copy the [emergency plan](../templates/emergency-plan.md) into a private local folder.
2. Verify your local emergency number through an official local source.
3. Identify two safe ways out where possible and choose a meeting place.
4. Find a recognised first-aid course. Note its date, cost, and accessibility.
5. Walk through the plan with your household; adapt it if you live alone.

## Self-check

1. What information should you give when asking for emergency help?
2. Why should you avoid approaching an unsafe scene?
3. What can hands-on training teach that reading cannot?

<details>
<summary>Suggested answers</summary>

1. Your location, what happened, the dangers present, and the information the dispatcher requests.
2. You could become another person needing rescue. Get professional help and follow instructions.
3. Supervised practice, feedback on technique, and skills appropriate to the situation and person.

</details>

**Done when:** you can find the plan without internet access and explain who you would contact and where you would go.
