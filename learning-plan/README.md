# Choose your learning pathway

[Curriculum](../README.md) · [Diagnostic](../curriculum/diagnostic.md) · [Dependency map](../curriculum/dependency-map.md)

Start with the diagnostic, choose two current priorities, and use the matching plan. Every pathway has 30 suggested 20-minute sessions, review checkpoints, and a three-session capstone. Skip introductory work only when you have evidence of the full criterion; otherwise use guidance and practise.

| Pathway | Useful when | Capstone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [General foundation](30-days.md) | Build a balanced foundation, then deepen the two skills your diagnostic identifies. | [C05](../capstones/busy-week.md) |
| [Independent living](independent-living.md) | Prepare to move, manage a household, and maintain essential services. | [C01](../capstones/moving-out.md) |
| [Money and administration](money-admin.md) | Build a dated money plan and handle bills, contracts, renewals, and advice routes. | [C03](../capstones/unexpected-expense.md) |
| [Digital safety](digital-safety.md) | Protect accounts and practise verification, recovery, and safe payment decisions. | [C04](../capstones/suspicious-payment.md) |
| [School to work](school-to-work.md) | Show truthful skill evidence, evaluate offers, communicate professionally, and organise work. | [C02](../capstones/first-job.md) |

Plans are queues you can adapt, not attendance requirements. Replace demonstrated skills with an unmet criterion, a fresh transfer task, or an optional branch. Review early skills on days 7, 14, 21, and 28 and schedule another check after the month for later skills. You can combine plans if your circumstances span several areas.
