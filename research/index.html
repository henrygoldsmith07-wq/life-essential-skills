Each morning this page collects new papers from preprint servers, medical databases, journals and science news, then turns the most useful into plain-language life skills, facts about the world and headlines — each linked to the original.

AI-written summaries. A language model drafts each summary from the paper’s title and abstract. It can be wrong. Preprints are not yet peer reviewed and one study rarely settles anything — open the source before relying on it. This is general education, not medical, financial or legal advice.

Where this comes from

Sources are queried through their free public APIs and feeds. alphaXiv and Elicit have no free public API: every arXiv paper links to its alphaXiv discussion, and Elicit’s corpus (Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex) is pulled directly. Cochrane reviews arrive through PubMed and link back to the Cochrane Library.
