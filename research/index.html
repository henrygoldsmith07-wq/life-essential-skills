Filters

AI-written summaries. Each paper is read by its own AI agent (from NVIDIA, Groq, OpenRouter, Cloudflare or Cerebras, named on every card), which writes the summary from the title and abstract. It can be wrong. Preprints are not yet peer reviewed, and one study rarely settles anything, so open the source before you rely on it. This is general education, not medical, financial or legal advice.

Where this comes from

Sources are queried through their free public APIs and feeds. alphaXiv and Elicit have no free public API: every arXiv paper links to its alphaXiv discussion, and Elicit’s corpus (Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex) is pulled directly. Cochrane reviews arrive through PubMed and link back to the Cochrane Library. “Free to read” links come from Unpaywall, and key-term definitions from Wikipedia.
