# S09 · An attractive offer and an unexplained deduction

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/09-work.md) · [Competencies](../domains/work.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

Fin is considering a fictional stockroom role. The sample pay rates and deductions are teaching data, not current legal rates or tax calculations.

## Materials

| Document | Extract |
| --- | --- |
| Advert | Accurate stock records; punctuality; teamwork; some weekend shifts |
| Offer | Estimated 80 hours per month at 12.50; exact schedule and guaranteed hours unclear |
| Payslip | Gross: 1,000; tax: 70; other stated statutory deduction: 30; pension: 40; net: 860 |
| Bank statement | Wage payment: 840 |
| Recruitment message | Send identity documents to a personal inbox before we discuss terms |

## Your task

Write one truthful evidence example for the advert and an interview question. Recalculate net pay and identify the discrepancy. Draft a professional clarification about hours, pay, verification, and secure handling of required documents. Check relevant current local employment guidance before judging compliance.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

The manager changes the hours and asks for unpaid mandatory training. Identify questions and an advice route; do not invent a legal entitlement from the sample.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

1,000 minus 70 minus 30 minus 40 is 860; the bank payment is 20 lower. Ask payroll to explain and reconcile it rather than guessing the cause or recalculating tax rules. Clarify guaranteed versus estimated hours, schedule, pay, training, notice, and other terms. Verify the organisation and document-handling process before sending sensitive information. A CV example should describe an actual task and result, including unpaid experience where relevant. Use Acas and the relevant current pay/tax guidance for real employment questions; fictional numbers do not establish legal compliance.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
