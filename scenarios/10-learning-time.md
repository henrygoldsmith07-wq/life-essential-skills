# S10 · A calendar with no travel time

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/10-learning-and-time.md) · [Competencies](../domains/learning-time.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

Dev has several commitments on Tuesday and Wednesday. The schedule must fit real time and energy.

## Materials

| Commitment | Time or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Tuesday work | 09:00–15:00 |
| Tuesday appointment | 15:20; travel from work takes 40 minutes |
| Coursework | Due Wednesday 12:00; estimated 60 minutes |
| Bill | Due Wednesday; 10-minute verification/payment task |
| Household task | 20 minutes; can move to Wednesday evening |
| Tuesday evening | 90 minutes available including food and rest |
| Learning review | 10 minutes, flexible |

## Your task

Identify the clash, choose priorities by deadline and consequences, and write a feasible plan including travel and basic needs. Draft a message to the affected appointment provider or supervisor. Schedule a later recall session and explain what to defer.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

The journey is delayed by another 30 minutes and the evening task takes twice as long. Replan and communicate early rather than filling the calendar with impossible overlaps.

## Assessment criteria

| Stage | Demonstrate | Competency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Identify fixed commitments and explain the difference between retrieval and rereading. | `learning-time.foundation` |

Apply the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md): correct reasoning, feasible action, verification, appropriate boundaries, and recorded help. Do not award a practical competency from a written plan alone.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

Leaving work at 15:00 reaches the appointment at 15:40, already 20 minutes late before disruption. Seek an agreed earlier departure, rearranged appointment, or other feasible option; do not assume permission. Protect the coursework and verified bill deadline, move the flexible household task, and allocate meals/rest and buffer realistically. If evening capacity is insufficient, identify an earlier available slot or contact the responsible person before the deadline. Retrieval requires answering from memory; schedule a later review. The changed travel and task estimates require a revised plan, not pretending the original fits.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
