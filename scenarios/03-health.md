# S03 · Missed appointment and a confident health post

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/03-health.md) · [Competencies](../domains/health.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

Sam has missed a routine appointment because an accessible bus was cancelled. They are unsure what to do after missing one dose of a prescribed medicine. There is no immediate emergency described.

## Materials

| Material | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Appointment note | Call the clinic through its official contact route to rearrange |
| Medicine packaging | Directions and patient leaflet available; medicine name intentionally omitted |
| Social post | A stranger says taking two doses tomorrow always fixes a missed dose |
| Access note | Sam needs step-free access and cannot assume every replacement service provides it |

## Your task

Prepare a clinic message, an accessible travel check, and a question for the pharmacist or prescribing team. Evaluate the social post. Explain which parts require professional advice rather than a calculation or diagnosis.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

An AI answer repeats the post and gives a convincing-looking citation you cannot locate. Revise your verification plan; do not use it to alter treatment.

## Assessment criteria

| Stage | Demonstrate | Competency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Identify routine, urgent, and emergency help routes from official local guidance. | `health.foundation` |
| Applied | Use a checklist to prepare a routine and appointment questions. | `health.applied` |
| Independent | Handle a missed appointment and an uncertain medicine instruction by contacting appropriate professionals. | `health.independent` |

Apply the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md): correct reasoning, feasible action, verification, appropriate boundaries, and recorded help. Do not award a practical competency from a written plan alone.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

Use the clinic's independently verified contact, explain the missed appointment and access need, and ask about rearranging and alternatives. Check operator accessibility and service information before travel. Consult the medicine's directions and ask a qualified pharmacist or clinician about the specific missed dose; the missing medicine details mean this exercise cannot determine a dose. Do not double a dose based on the post or AI answer. An untraceable citation is not evidence. Use local routine, urgent, or emergency routes if circumstances require them; this scenario does not assess clinical triage.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
