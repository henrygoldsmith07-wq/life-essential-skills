# S05 · The last-minute favour

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/05-relationships.md) · [Competencies](../domains/relationships.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

Morgan repeatedly asks Lee for lifts with little notice. Lee cannot always change their work and study arrangements.

## Materials

| Material | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Message from Morgan | You helped before, so you have to drive me tonight |
| Lee's commitment | Study session 18:00–19:00; transport costs need planning |
| Morgan's explanation | The original travel plan fell through |
| Relationship context | No threat is initially described |

## Your task

Draft Lee's reply using an observation, effect, and request or boundary. Reflect Morgan's concern without agreeing to the demand. Propose a feasible alternative if there is one, and respect that either person can refuse.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

Morgan begins threatening Lee. Explain how the response changes when the situation is unsafe; do not treat threats as a negotiation exercise.

## Assessment criteria

| Stage | Demonstrate | Competency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Distinguish an observation from a motive claim and recognise that consent can change. | `relationships.foundation` |
| Applied | Use a script to make a request, reflect a reply, and define a boundary. | `relationships.applied` |
| Independent | Handle a realistic disagreement with listening, a clear request, and a workable boundary. | `relationships.independent` |
| Advanced Scenario | Adapt to refusal or escalating behaviour, choosing a safe pause or appropriate support. | `relationships.advanced-scenario` |

Apply the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md): correct reasoning, feasible action, verification, appropriate boundaries, and recorded help. Do not award a practical competency from a written plan alone.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

For example: I understand your travel plan fell through. Last-minute requests disrupt my study and budget. I cannot drive tonight. For future lifts, please ask earlier; I may still need to say no. A previous favour does not create current agreement. Explore another safe transport option only if feasible. If threats appear, prioritise safety and appropriate support rather than trying to win the conversation or impose a discussion. The assessment is the clarity and safety of the response, not whether Morgan accepts it.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
