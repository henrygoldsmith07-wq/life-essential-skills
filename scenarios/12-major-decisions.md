# S12 · Two homes and a disrupted route

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/12-major-decisions.md) · [Competencies](../domains/major-decisions.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

Nia compares fictional rental adverts in Wales. The extracts are incomplete teaching materials, not valid occupation contracts.

## Materials

| Option | Monthly rent | Utilities estimate | Transport | Commute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 650 | 120 | 110 | 55 minutes |
| B | 730 | 100 | 40 | 20 minutes |

Advert A requests a payment before viewing and says repairs are the occupant's responsibility without further detail. Advert B has an unclear deposit and written terms. Neither says whether step-free access is available.

Transport note: route A's last bus leaves 20:30; Nia sometimes finishes at 21:00. Course advert: an additional paid qualification promises guaranteed employment without evidence.

## Your task

Compare stated monthly costs and practical access/transport needs. List contract, payment-verification, and repair questions before choosing. Find maintained Wales housing guidance and a relevant advice service. Assess the course claim and identify the appropriate local authority or community service for a practical need.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

Income falls and route B is suspended for a week. Recompare feasible options and a reversible next step; do not assume cheap emergency transport exists.

## Assessment criteria

| Stage | Demonstrate | Competency |

Apply the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md): correct reasoning, feasible action, verification, appropriate boundaries, and recorded help. Do not award a practical competency from a written plan alone.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

A totals 880 and B totals 870 before other living costs and upfront payments. B is 10 less on these estimates and has a shorter commute, but incomplete terms and access needs remain unresolved. A's last bus cannot support a 21:00 finish without a verified feasible alternative. Verify the landlord/agent and payment request; ask about written terms, deposit, responsibilities, condition, and accessibility. Use Welsh Government renting guidance and Shelter Cymru for Wales; England's rules cannot be substituted. Seek evidence for the course's job promise. If circumstances change, revisit affordability, live transport and support, and consider viewing or obtaining advice before committing. Identify services by function, not political preference.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
