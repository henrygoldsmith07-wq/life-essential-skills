# S01 · Outage with an access need

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/01-emergencies.md) · [Competencies](../domains/emergencies.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

A storm has cut power in a block of flats. Rowan has limited mobility. A neighbour wants to investigate a dark, flooded basement. No one is injured at present.

## Materials

| Material | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Household note | Phone battery 18%; torch available; internet router has no power |
| Building note | Lift unavailable; stair access needs assistance planned in advance |
| Local alert | Flooding possible; check the current official local alert |
| Contact plan | Trusted contact available initially; building manager contact stored offline |
| Training record | No recent practical first-aid training |

## Your task

Write a safe first-five-minute sequence. Explain what you would tell the neighbour, how Rowan's access needs change the plan, which contacts to verify, and what you would prepare before another outage. Find a recognised training pathway rather than attempting medical techniques.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

The trusted contact stops answering and the phone loses signal. Revise the communication plan. If someone develops an immediate life-threatening emergency, state the verified local emergency route; do not diagnose or enter a hazard.

## Assessment criteria

| Stage | Demonstrate | Competency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Identify an appropriate emergency help route and explain why scene safety comes first. | `emergencies.foundation` |
| Applied | Use a checklist to make an offline household plan including access needs. | `emergencies.applied` |
| Independent | Respond to the outage scenario with a safe sequence and verified support routes. | `emergencies.independent` |
| Advanced Scenario | Adapt the plan for a failed phone and an additional access need; identify professional limits. | `emergencies.advanced-scenario` |

Apply the [assessment rules](../curriculum/mastery.md): correct reasoning, feasible action, verification, appropriate boundaries, and recorded help. Do not award a practical competency from a written plan alone.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

Keep everyone away from the flooded basement; use the torch and assess safety from a safe place. Do not improvise lift or electrical repairs. Use the existing building/access plan and independently verified manager, utility, and emergency-authority routes as appropriate. The plan needs an offline meeting/contact fallback and agreed assistance arrangements, not an assumption that Rowan can take the stairs. Immediate danger takes priority over paperwork. A future plan should address charging, light, local hazards, access needs, and recognised training. In Wales, use the Wales pack to verify emergency and utility routes.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
