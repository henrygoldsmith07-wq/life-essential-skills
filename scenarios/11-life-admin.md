# S11 · The phone deal and the renewal

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/11-life-admin.md) · [Competencies](../domains/life-admin.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

Jamie compares two fictional mobile offers while dealing with a subscription renewal. Contract extracts are invented and do not establish legal rights.

## Materials

| Extract | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| Phone A | 24 monthly payments of 18; setup fee 20; future price changes not explained |
| Phone B | 12 monthly payments of 30; no setup fee; future price changes not explained |
| Streaming | 9 per month; renews on day 30; stated notice requirement 7 calendar days |
| Today | Day 25 of the same fictional month |
| Bill | Unexpected duplicate 9 charge; provider dispute process available on its official site |

## Your task

Calculate minimum stated committed cost for both phone offers and explain why different durations affect comparison. Check the stated streaming notice deadline. Draft a cancellation/clarification request and duplicate-charge complaint, and plan evidence and follow-up using verified channels.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

The provider says the duplicate is valid and sends an unfamiliar payment link. Plan the next verification and advice steps without asserting an automatic refund right.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

A's stated minimum is 24 × 18 + 20 = 452; B's is 12 × 30 = 360. Different term lengths and unknown price changes mean this is not a complete like-for-like comparison. Under the extract's stated arithmetic, notice would be needed by day 23; day 25 is too late for that stated deadline. Ask the provider what happens next and check current local rights—do not assume the sample notice clause is legally conclusive. Record the duplicate charge, request an explanation and appropriate remedy, keep confirmation and dates privately, and follow the verified complaint/advice route. Verify a new payment link independently.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
