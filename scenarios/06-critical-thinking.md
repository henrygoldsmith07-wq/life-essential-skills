# S06 · A dramatic statistic and an AI summary

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/06-thinking.md) · [Competencies](../domains/critical-thinking.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

A social post claims a study app doubles exam success. An AI summary repeats the claim and calls the app proven.

## Materials

| Extract | Fictional information |
| --- | --- |
| Report table | App group: 20 passes from 100; comparison group: 10 passes from 100 |
| Method note | Participants selected their own group; prior attainment differs; no random assignment |
| Social post | Buy today; everyone who wants success uses this |
| AI citation | A plausible paper title with no retrievable author, publisher, or URL |
| Video | A supposed teacher endorsement; provenance not established |

## Your task

Calculate pass rates, relative difference, and absolute percentage-point difference. State what the design can and cannot establish. Write a source-verification plan for the AI citation and video, then a conclusion with uncertainty.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

The video is reposted by a familiar account and the source table is removed. Explain what remains verified and what you would need before purchasing or sharing the claim.

## Assessment criteria

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

Pass rates are 20% and 10%. The app group's rate is twice the comparison rate (100% relative increase) and 10 percentage points higher. This self-selected comparison does not establish that the app caused the difference. Prior attainment and other differences may matter. Seek the original report and independently verifiable citation; a realistic video or familiar repost is not proof of authorship. The sales pressure is not evidence. If the table is no longer accessible, keep the documented limits visible and label unsupported claims unverified. These numbers are invented for practice, not research findings.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
