# S08 · A food budget and a leaking appliance

[Scenario library](README.md) · [Guide](../guides/08-home.md) · [Competencies](../domains/home.md)

**Time:** about 20 minutes; split practical tasks across sessions. All people, documents, amounts, and events are fictional. Use local official guidance for real situations.

## Context

Ari is planning two simple meals for a small household. The washing machine has leaked near an electrical socket. There is no injury described.

## Materials

| Shopping item | Price | Need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tinned beans, two tins | 1.60 | Protein source |
| Bread | 1.20 | Starch |
| Frozen vegetables | 1.50 | Vegetables |
| Fruit | 1.20 | Additional food |
| Washing detergent | 2.00 | Not needed this week; some remains |

Food budget: 6.00. Household note: fridge works; ingredient/package instructions available; one laundry label says hand wash. Repair responsibility must be checked in the household agreement.

## Your task

Choose a shopping list and calculate its cost. Describe food preparation/storage checks. Plan laundry and household tasks. Draft a repair report and explain what you must not attempt around water and electricity.

Produce a short action plan, relevant calculations, and any message requested. For an independent attempt, close the guide and do not open the explanation. Record any help used; practical actions need separate evidence.

## Advanced variation

The fridge stops working before food is stored. Revise the shopping/meal plan using current official food-safety guidance, without assuming affected food remains safe.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after your attempt</summary>

The four food items total 5.50, leaving 0.50; adding unnecessary detergent would exceed the food budget. Check allergies, instructions, preparation hygiene, and current storage guidance. Separate the hand-wash item and follow labels. Keep clear of the leaking appliance and electrical hazard; obtain appropriate help through verified responsible contacts rather than experimenting with power or repairs. Describe the location and fault without approaching danger to gather evidence. With failed refrigeration, reassess storage and food safety using official advice; choose suitable alternatives rather than declaring food safe by appearance.

Other solutions can meet the criteria when they are correct, feasible, and safely scoped. Record what you would change before a fresh delayed attempt.

</details>
