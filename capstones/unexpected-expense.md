# C03 · Unexpected expense

[Capstones](README.md) · [Mastery rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) · [Evidence sheet](../templates/assessment-record.md)

**Time:** three sessions of about 20 minutes. These are fictional multi-skill assessments. Use current local guidance for real decisions.

## Context

Rae needs an essential device repair but has limited cash. Compare options without overlooking bills or the timing of income.

## Materials

| Item | Amount/timing |
| --- | --- |
| Available money today | 300 |
| Essential bills before next income | 240 |
| Repair | 180 today |
| Next income | 600 in ten days; later commitments not yet listed |
| Offer A | Borrow 180; fee 10 now; three repayments of 65 after next payday |
| Offer B | Borrow 180; four repayments of 52; first due in five days |
| Social claim | Guaranteed approval means guaranteed affordability |
| Support | Legitimate advice/provider arrangement may be available; eligibility unknown |

## Your task

Calculate the current shortfall and total cost of each offer. Explain timing, missing terms, and why the claim does not prove affordability. Outline a verification/support conversation, private evidence log, and small next step to reduce overwhelm.

Session 1: analyse and verify. Session 2: produce calculations, messages, and plan. Session 3: review your reasoning and handle the variation. Record any prompts or help; close the explanation for an independent attempt.

## Advanced variation

The next income is delayed another week. Reassess the choices and contact plan; do not assume a lender or provider must offer an arrangement.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after completing your attempt</summary>

After reserving 240, only 60 remains; the repair creates a 120 shortfall. A costs 10 + 3 × 65 = 205, a borrowing cost of 25; B costs 4 × 52 = 208, a cost of 28. A's fee is immediate; B requires a payment before the stated next income. Future commitments are missing, so neither can be declared affordable. Check the repair's necessity, timing, possible alternatives, complete terms, and legitimate advice or arrangements. Approval is a lender decision, not proof that repayment fits Rae's life. Break the task into gathering bills and making one verified enquiry. A delayed income requires revisiting dates and assumptions early.

There may be several defensible choices. Assess the reasoning, verification, calculations, and feasible actions rather than agreement with a preferred life choice.

</details>
