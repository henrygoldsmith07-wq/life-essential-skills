# C05 · Busy week

[Capstones](README.md) · [Mastery rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) · [Evidence sheet](../templates/assessment-record.md)

**Time:** three sessions of about 20 minutes. These are fictional multi-skill assessments. Use current local guidance for real decisions.

## Context

Em is managing work/study, an appointment, a bill, and household tasks. The week should remain feasible when something changes.

## Materials

| Commitment | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Monday work | 09:00–16:00 |
| Monday appointment | 16:15; travel 35 minutes |
| Tuesday assignment | Due 12:00; 90 minutes of work left |
| Monday evening | 120 minutes free, including food and rest |
| Bill | Due Tuesday; amount already reserved; payment request must be verified |
| Laundry | 30 minutes of active work; can wait until Wednesday |
| Transport | Usual return service may be cancelled; alternative needs verification |
| Support | A trusted person could discuss task sharing, but has not agreed |

## Your task

Create a realistic dated plan and identify what cannot fit. Draft an appointment/commitment message and a task-sharing request. Verify bill and journey routes, plan basic food/rest, and schedule a later skills reassessment. Explain what you will defer and why.

Session 1: analyse and verify. Session 2: produce calculations, messages, and plan. Session 3: review your reasoning and handle the variation. Record any prompts or help; close the explanation for an independent attempt.

## Advanced variation

The appointment cannot be moved and the assignment takes another hour. Replan, communicate early, and identify the unresolved constraint instead of claiming everything fits.

An assisted row is useful practice but not independent demonstration. A capstone does not automatically award every domain stage: check the domain's full criterion and any required practical evidence. The variation supports advanced assessment only for the corresponding adaptation criterion.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after completing your attempt</summary>

The appointment cannot be reached until 16:35 if work ends at 16:00. Seek an agreed earlier departure or another feasible arrangement; do not assume either is available. Ninety minutes of assignment work leaves only 30 evening minutes for food/rest, bill verification, and other needs, so explore an earlier slot or timely contact about constraints. Defer laundry to Wednesday and request help only with consent. Keep reserved bill money separate, use official contact/payment routes, and verify live travel options. The extra assignment hour creates a new capacity gap. A sound answer can identify that no feasible plan is currently established and seek help or renegotiation; it need not pretend to solve every constraint.

There may be several defensible choices. Assess the reasoning, verification, calculations, and feasible actions rather than agreement with a preferred life choice.

</details>
