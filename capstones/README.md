# Capstone challenges

[Curriculum](../README.md) · [Scenarios](../scenarios/README.md) · [Pathways](../learning-plan/README.md)

Combine skills in realistic decisions. Start after practising the relevant Applied competencies, or use a capstone with help to discover what you need next. Written judgement is assessed separately from hands-on skills.

| Challenge | Domains working together |
| --- | --- |
| [C01 · Moving out](moving-out.md) | Money, Everyday independence, Life administration, Major decisions and community, Emergencies |
| [C02 · First job](first-job.md) | Work and earning, Money, Relationships and communication, Life administration, Learning and time |
| [C03 · Unexpected expense](unexpected-expense.md) | Money, Critical thinking and AI literacy, Life administration, Emotional wellbeing |
| [C04 · Suspicious payment request](suspicious-payment.md) | Digital safety, Critical thinking and AI literacy, Relationships and communication, Money, Life administration |
| [C05 · Busy week](busy-week.md) | Learning and time, Emotional wellbeing, Life administration, Relationships and communication, Everyday independence, Major decisions and community |

Use the per-challenge rubric and [assessment record](../templates/assessment-record.md). Retain calculations and messages with fictional details, record help used, and repeat later with changed materials. A complete plan may include an unresolved constraint and an appropriate help request; invented certainty does not count as mastery.
