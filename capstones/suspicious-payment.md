# C04 · Suspicious payment request

[Capstones](README.md) · [Mastery rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) · [Evidence sheet](../templates/assessment-record.md)

**Time:** three sessions of about 20 minutes. These are fictional multi-skill assessments. Use current local guidance for real decisions.

## Context

A fictional club treasurer receives a message apparently from the chair requesting an urgent payment. Assess it without assuming either fraud or authenticity.

## Materials

```text
Please send 450 to the replacement supplier account now.
Keep this between us so the event is not cancelled.
My phone is broken; use the new number in this message.
An attached voice recording says the payment is approved.
```

The verified club balance is 900. Already committed bills total 650. The original supplier invoice lists other account details. No independent confirmation is available yet. No real account identifiers are supplied.

## Your task

Calculate uncommitted funds and identify the decision gaps. Write a safe verification sequence and a concise message through an independently established channel. Explain the limits of the recording as evidence and what to record if a payment or code has already been shared.

Session 1: analyse and verify. Session 2: produce calculations, messages, and plan. Session 3: review your reasoning and handle the variation. Record any prompts or help; close the explanation for an independent attempt.

## Advanced variation

The sender knows genuine event details and calls with a familiar-sounding voice. Explain what verification still needs to happen.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after completing your attempt</summary>

Uncommitted funds are 900 − 650 = 250. The requested 450 is 200 more, even if genuine. Hold action while independently verifying the chair's identity/authority, the invoice, and the account change using trusted existing contacts. Urgency, secrecy, genuine details, and a realistic voice do not replace verification. A good message states the discrepancy and asks for confirmation through the agreed process. If money or codes were shared, use relevant official bank/account support and current local fraud routes promptly; record dates and references privately. Do not promise a particular recovery outcome.

There may be several defensible choices. Assess the reasoning, verification, calculations, and feasible actions rather than agreement with a preferred life choice.

</details>
