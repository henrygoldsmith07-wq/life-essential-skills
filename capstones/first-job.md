# C02 · First job

[Capstones](README.md) · [Mastery rules](../curriculum/mastery.md) · [Evidence sheet](../templates/assessment-record.md)

**Time:** three sessions of about 20 minutes. These are fictional multi-skill assessments. Use current local guidance for real decisions.

## Context

Jules is applying for a fictional weekend assistant job while completing coursework. Show truthful evidence and check the offer before accepting.

## Materials

| Document | Extract |
| --- | --- |
| Advert | Customer communication, accurate records, reliability |
| Experience | Jules organised a club rota and checked attendance records; no paid experience |
| Offer | 60 hours/month at 13; rota changes require clarification; mandatory training terms absent |
| Payslip | Gross 780; deductions 40 + 20 + 30; stated net 690 |
| Bank | Payment 680 |
| Calendar | Interview Tuesday 16:00; class ends 15:30; travel takes 45 minutes |

Pay rates and deductions are fictional. Age, legal compliance, tax rules, and employment status have not been established.

## Your task

Write a targeted application paragraph and interview answer using the club experience. Draft three questions about the offer and a professional message about the interview clash. Reconcile pay, identify local employment/tax sources, and create a record/reminder plan for starting work.

Session 1: analyse and verify. Session 2: produce calculations, messages, and plan. Session 3: review your reasoning and handle the variation. Record any prompts or help; close the explanation for an independent attempt.

## Advanced variation

The employer changes the interview to tomorrow and asks for identity documents through an unverified personal account. Revise the plan and verification steps.

## Suggested solution

<details>
<summary>Open after completing your attempt</summary>

The experience can show organisation and accurate record keeping without pretending it was paid work. Gross is 60 × 13 = 780; 780 − 40 − 20 − 30 = 690, so the payment is 10 short. Ask payroll to reconcile it rather than deciding why. The earliest arrival after class is 16:15, so seek an agreed change or alternative; do not claim the 16:00 slot fits. Ask about hours, rota, training, pay, notice, and secure document handling. Verify the organisation and official recipient before sharing sensitive documents. Use current Acas/HMRC and Wales or relevant careers guidance. A changed interview date requires checking capacity and a clear response, not automatically sacrificing other commitments.

There may be several defensible choices. Assess the reasoning, verification, calculations, and feasible actions rather than agreement with a preferred life choice.

</details>
