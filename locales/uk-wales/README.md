This pack combines the resources below with the shared UK pack. It keeps Wales-specific healthcare and housing separate from England guidance. Pages may provide English and Welsh versions; use the format and language that work for you.

## Immediate and routine support

For a life-threatening medical emergency in Wales, call **999** and follow the call handler's instructions. For urgent health advice, use **NHS 111 Wales**; its current guidance also explains urgent mental-health support through **111, option 2**. Verify the linked service for current scope and access options. Learning tasks must not delay getting help.

## Use the right route

- **Housing:** Welsh Government's renting-homes guidance and Shelter Cymru. Do not substitute an England tenancy extract for Wales occupation-contract advice.
- **Money/consumer/tax/employment:** use shared MoneyHelper, CMA, HMRC, and Acas resources where their scope fits; Citizens Advice Wales and Advicelink provide relevant support/signposting.
- **Legal/advice:** Citizens Advice Wales, Shelter Cymru for housing, and the shared legal-aid information. Eligibility and issue scope need checking; this is not a promise of representation.
- **Education/work:** Careers Wales for options and careers support; verify entry requirements and course claims with the relevant provider and recognised authority.
- **Local government/community:** use the shared council finder and official council site for the responsible service, library, waste, or local arrangements.
- **Transport:** Traveline Cymru for planning, then the operator's current fares, accessibility, and disruption information. A timetable is not a guarantee that a service is running.
- **Hazards/utilities:** Natural Resources Wales for floods; shared electricity-network guidance for power cuts; Wales & West Utilities for gas services in its territory. Do not experiment with hazardous systems.
