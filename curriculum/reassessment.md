# Fresh delayed reassessment cards

[Mastery rules](mastery.md) · [Progress tracker](../templates/progress-tracker.md)

Use these after a few days, a week, or several weeks. Close the original solution. Produce a new answer, then assess it against the domain criterion and verify facts/calculations. Ask a partner to change another detail where possible. These are fictional prompts, not real-world advice.

| Domain | Fresh situation | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergencies | An evacuation route in a new building is unavailable and a contact cannot be reached | Safe sequence, access needs, verified professional route, and offline alternative |
| Money | Income 1,450; planned outgoings 1,300; repair 210 before payday; opening available funds 90 | Monthly and timed gaps, no assumed extra money, appropriate support questions |
| Health | A clinic changes venue and an online post contradicts medicine directions | Verified contact and access plan; no improvised treatment |
| Wellbeing | Two tasks compete, the first support contact is unavailable, and distress is increasing | Manageable action, basic needs, fallback professional help |
| Relationships | A friend declines your request and asks you to keep discussing it late at night | Listening, respect for refusal, a boundary within your control, and safety awareness |
| Critical thinking | Group A: 30/200; group B: 20/200; selection method unknown | Rates, absolute/relative difference, study limitations, and verification |
| Digital safety | A QR code in an unexpected message asks you to re-enter account credentials | Independent official route; no assumption that a QR code is safe |
| Home | The cooker is unavailable and the laundry label differs from the usual load | Feasible alternative meal, safe storage, label-following, no hazardous repair |
| Work | Gross 900; deductions 60 and 45; bank receipt 785; hours unclear | Correct reconciliation, professional questions, current local advice |
| Learning and time | Work ends 14:30; appointment 15:00; journey 45 minutes; bill due today | Detects clash, realistic priorities, early communication, buffer |
| Life administration | A 14/month service adds a 12 setup fee and an unclear renewal | Stated minimum for a defined term, missing conditions, verified cancellation questions |
| Major decisions | Option A costs 780 plus 130 travel; B costs 850 plus 35; route B disrupted | Correct totals, access/timing, verified backup, provisional decision |

Do not compare answers to the original scenario solution when the inputs have changed. Verify arithmetic independently, consult the current source for jurisdiction-dependent facts, and use the criterion as the rubric. If a key fact is missing, naming it and seeking appropriate clarification can be the correct response.

If a task is retained, record the evidence and schedule the next useful review. If help is needed, mark that result and repeat a smaller exercise. Never erase an earlier successful demonstration; add the new result so progress remains honest.

## Scheduled fresh checks

The [learner dashboard](../learner/index.html) derives next-review dates from configurable policies and chooses an unseen exposure group. A failed or assisted check preserves past evidence and schedules another return. The cards above remain useful manual prompts; do not award retained transfer from a memorised original answer.
