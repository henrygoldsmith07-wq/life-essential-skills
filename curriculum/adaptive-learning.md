# Your evidence and your next task

[Start](../START-HERE.md) · [Learner dashboard](../learner/index.html) · [Text diagnostic](diagnostic.md)

## A useful learning loop

Start with a fictional diagnostic. Choose a skill relevant to your situation, learn the missing step, practise with support, and attempt a new scenario before opening feedback. Record which criteria your original response actually met, including solving help. The next task follows that evidence. Come back for a delayed check using different materials.

Reading and confidence never award competence. A safe conclusion that information is insufficient can be better evidence than a confident invented answer.

## What gets recorded

- **Knowledge:** a correct explanation and basic output in a familiar kind of task.
- **Guided practice:** a useful attempt with solving prompts, a checklist, a person or AI.
- **Independent performance:** every essential criterion on a fresh task without solving help.
- **Adaptation:** a feasible revised response after constraints or context change.
- **Retention:** another independent demonstration later, on unseen material.

Knowledge is counted separately from independent or adaptive judgement. Access support, including a scribe, screen reader, translation and extra time, does not reduce the rating. An official source supplying relevant facts can be part of independent verification; a source supplying the answer is solving help.

The dashboard supports self-review. These records are provisional educational evidence, not certification. For important assessments, ask an assessor to check the original output against the rubric. Written emergency, meal or device plans do not prove CPR, cooking or actual technical configuration. Practical requirements remain separate, and the two existing practical roll-ups require an assessor's observed record.

## Why a task is recommended

The rules use this order: due delayed reviews; unresolved safety, privacy, consent or jurisdiction errors; an attempted chosen goal or prerequisite; other not-yet criteria; assisted criteria; unassessed chosen goals; remaining pathway skills. Ties use the earliest relevant date, pathway domain order and stable competency order. Every suggestion explains the evidence and prerequisite decisions.

Only eligible locality and prerequisite routes are suggested. Your chosen goal also brings its missing prerequisite skills into the queue. Recent error tags select a fresh variant focused on the missed step when one is available; they never produce an opaque numerical score. Upcoming review dates appear on Today and in Progress.

Foundation and independent items can share materials. The engine tracks **exposure groups**, so changing the item ID does not turn a known answer into an unseen test. When fresh variants run out, it says fresh materials are needed. Practising a known case remains useful but cannot count as transfer or retention.

## Returning later

Default successful-review intervals are 3, 7, 21 and 60 days; safety-focused skills use 2, 7, 21 and 45 days. Basic knowledge checks use 14, 45 and 120 days, and do not repeat separately while a current full independent performance already exercises the same basic output. This does not award any unrecorded knowledge evidence. These are configurable practical defaults, not scientific promises or a claim of universal optimal spacing. A failed or assisted attempt resets the review progression and schedules a shorter return. Earlier successful demonstrations stay in the history.

Dates are calendar dates in your local browser; scheduling arithmetic uses date-only UTC internally to avoid daylight-saving shifts. Retention needs an earlier demonstration, a later date, unchanged assessment-version provenance and unseen exposure groups. A newly edited item is not automatically a fresh transfer case.

## Local guidance and privacy

UK unspecified never silently selects England. Choose Wales for Welsh housing and service routes. Other nations are represented in the jurisdiction model but do not yet have complete learner packs; verify the appropriate nation before relying on local law, healthcare or services. Do not transfer a general budgeting principle into an assertion about another country's benefits or lending rules.

Your browser stores educational evidence only. Responses stay in memory and are discarded when you leave the task. Download/import a local JSON backup if useful. Do not enter passwords, financial account data, real medical records, identity documents or private contacts. No analytics, learner comparisons, streaks or leaderboards are used.

Record only the criteria demonstrated by each output; a capstone is not an automatic domain award.
