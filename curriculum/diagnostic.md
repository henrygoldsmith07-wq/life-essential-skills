# A practical starting diagnostic

[Start here](../START-HERE.md) · [Pathways](../learning-plan/README.md) · [Mastery](mastery.md)

Use up to three 20-minute sessions. You can begin with the most relevant rows; the diagnostic must not delay urgent help. Use fictional information and record outputs locally.

## Try a small task

| Domain | Diagnostic task | Check for evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergencies | Outline a safe response to an outage in an unfamiliar building | Scene safety, verified help, access needs, and an offline fallback |
| Money | Income 1,200; essentials 900; other costs 150; saving 75. Explain the remainder and what a bill before payday changes | Correct arithmetic and awareness of timing |
| Health | An appointment is missed and a medicine instruction is unclear. Identify your next contact | Appropriate verified service; no invented diagnosis or dose |
| Wellbeing | A task feels overwhelming. Choose one small step and a specific support request | Manageable action and appropriate help |
| Relationships | Rewrite “You never care” as an observation and request | Specific behaviour, clear request, respect for refusal |
| Critical thinking | A risk changes from 2 in 100 to 3 in 100. Explain absolute and relative change | Denominator, correct calculation, and limits of the claim |
| Digital safety | An unexpected message asks for a payment and an authentication code | Independent verification and protection of secrets |
| Home | Plan a simple meal and explain when a leaking appliance needs qualified help | Feasible plan, food instructions, and repair safety limits |
| Work | Write a truthful example matching a sample job requirement | Specific action and outcome; no invented experience |
| Learning and time | Work ends 16:00; appointment 16:20; travel 40 minutes. Plan a response | Detects clash and seeks a feasible arrangement |
| Life administration | Explain the questions needed before accepting a recurring contract | Cost, duration, renewal, cancellation, responsibilities |
| Major decisions | Compare two options with different ongoing costs and uncertain access | Full costs, practical needs, missing facts, and verification |

Check each output against the domain's [observable criteria](dependency-map.md), then use its scenario for a fuller assessment. A brief diagnostic is a screening task: it does not establish practical or advanced competence on its own. Record the highest stage whose full criterion actually has evidence.

## Choose priorities

1. **Needs attention now:** an immediate safety concern, essential bill, imminent deadline, or essential access need. Use appropriate services first.
2. **Useful this month:** a move, job application, new responsibility, or repeated difficulty. Choose the matching pathway.
3. **Supporting skill missing:** check the dependency map and practise the relevant prerequisite.
4. **Already demonstrated:** skip the introductory session, try a fresh scenario or capstone, and book a retention check.

Select two priority domains and one supporting domain. Record the reason, demonstration evidence, help used, and next review date in your [progress tracker](../templates/progress-tracker.md). You do not need a single total score or a ranking against other learners.
