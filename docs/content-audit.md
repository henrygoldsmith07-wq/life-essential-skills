# Content and architecture audit · 2026-10-03

## Scope

Reviewed the repository tree, twelve guides and domain pages, diagnostic, mastery and dependency rules, twelve scenarios, five capstones, five 30-day pathways, reassessment cards, templates, source registry, UK/Wales packs, scripts, nineteen existing regression tests and both workflows before extending their architecture. The original working tree matched the remote baseline. The original curriculum and identifiers remain intact.

## Findings and changes

- Domain stages were useful summaries but could hide weaknesses in budgeting, timing, borrowing or other distinct abilities. Added grouped observable subskills and explicit legacy criterion roll-ups rather than replacing IDs or awarding all domain skills at once.
- One scenario per domain could reward memorisation. Added realistic priority banks and distinct smaller-family cases, exposure groups across stages, feedback-after-attempt and delayed unseen review. Cases intentionally contain missing facts and sometimes no established feasible solution.
- General background and local authority references were mixed. Added typed source scope and explicit use roles, removed England housing/careers references from assessment lists, and declared S12/C01 as Wales. Retained valuable original teaching references with scoped uses.
- Existing practical caveats were strong but could be lost in automation. The engine explicitly withholds practical roll-ups pending assessor observations; written planning remains limited to judgement.
- Existing scenario arithmetic was retained after checking: S02 monthly allocation1435/surplus165, pre-pay minimum-35 and repair gap-255; S06 percentage-point versus relative differences; S09 net860 versus receipt840; S11 452 versus360 over unequal durations; S12 housing880 versus870. Capstone budgets, pay differences and travel clashes remained coherent.
- Existing task outcomes could imply completion from a guide alone. Added learner navigation and evidence explanations separating knowledge, help, independent performance, adaptation and retention. Self-review is labelled provisional.
- Source HTTP checks did not reveal substantive change. Added bounded main-text fingerprints and per-source review intervals with manual review signals; neither CI nor the monitor silently certifies guidance or refreshes review dates.
- Per-test full repository copies made local mutation tests unnecessarily slow. Tests now share an isolated fixture and restore only mutated files between cases, preserving the original rejection assertions.

## Editorial limits

Written simulations do not certify physical skills, clinical decisions or legal validity. Additional national learner packs need local review before release. Variant axes and benchmark rationales support maintainers, but automated duplicate detection cannot establish psychometric equivalence or replace editorial/assessor calibration. The evaluation framework is descriptive until a properly designed study supplies stronger evidence.
