Your setup

UK does not mean England. Nation-specific assessments appear only when their guidance fits. Choose Wales for Welsh housing and local services.

Learners on this browser

Each learner keeps a separate evidence slot on this browser — useful for a family computer or a shared tablet. Switching never mixes evidence. Removing a learner deletes only their slot.

Your evidence stays here

This browser stores task IDs, dates, criteria, help and error categories. Your written answer is temporary and is never saved. No login, analytics or uploads.

Use fictional data. Keep passwords, bank details, identity documents, medical records and private contacts outside this tool. Self-reviewed evidence is a learning record; ask an assessor to verify important claims.

Only supported educational evidence can be imported. Back up locally before changing browsers or clearing browser storage. Practical observations can be imported only as assessor-reviewed records.

Encrypted backup with a passphrase

A plain backup file can be read by anyone who opens it — on a shared computer, in an inbox, or on a lost drive. A passphrase-locked file opens only with the passphrase. Use one you will remember: a forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered.

THINK IT THROUGH

Work through a decision

From the decision journal: name the question, compare options against what matters most, note your confidence, choose — then review later against what actually happened. The weighted comparison supports your judgement; it never picks for you. This workspace is yours alone — it is never imported as evidence.
